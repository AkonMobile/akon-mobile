## 🛠️ Step-by-Step User Guide: How to Get Started

Setting up an account and publishing your first item or listing on Akon Mobile is fast and straightforward. Follow these quick steps:


### Step 1: Account Creation
Begin by creating a user profile on our website. Visit the [Akon Mobile Sign-In Page](https://www.akonmobile.com/sign-in/) to create a free account or log in using your existing credentials.

### Step 2: Choose Your Listing Category
Once logged in, click on the [Add Listing Button](https://www.akonmobile.com/add-listing/) on the top menu to select the category that best matches your target audience:
* Choose **Buy & Sell** for mobile phones, devices, and physical items.
* Choose **Jobs & Services** for hiring or offering professional work.
* Choose **Directory** for adding company details and website URL citations.

### Step 3: Complete the Form & Publish
Provide accurate title descriptions, clear images, location details, and pricing. Review your details and hit **Submit**. Your listing will be indexed promptly on the [Akon Mobile Marketplace](https://www.akonmobile.com/all-listings/).
